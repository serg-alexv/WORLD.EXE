## Bounded development team
Read DevTeam/README.md before agent work. Generated proposals are untrusted, never execution or acceptance evidence. Keep credentials, model weights, provider correspondence, candidate responses and player feedback in private WORLD.EXE-ops, outside Git. No agent may change its own authority, acceptance tests, signing permissions, scheduled tasks, Apple resources or release gates. A human-directed implementation session may review and apply a proposal; scheduled observers may not. Trae remains an interactive lane.
